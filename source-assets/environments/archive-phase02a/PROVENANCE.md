# Archive Phase 02A — original material source chain

Recorded 2026-11-11 for the repository owner's explicit environment-polish instruction. The six-swatch master was generated through the built-in OpenAI `image_gen` tool from the original institutional-material brief in [material-prompt.txt](material-prompt.txt). No image, third-party texture, character sheet, stock photograph, named artist or other game's media was supplied as a generation input. The palette, institutional purpose and physical surface requirements come from this project's art direction.

The exact delivered PNG is retained as `material-master.png`, including its embedded metadata. The tool output filename and SHA-256 are recorded in [generation.json](generation.json); the backend model/version, provider seed and cost were not exposed. A local filename identifies an actual tool output, not a fabricated public job URL. The raw source was directly inspected before normalization.

`utils/build-archive-materials.mjs` deterministically crops the six swatches, area-samples them to 256×256, repairs opposing edges through a 16px feather, removes source hue and controls contrast. Each finish produces an sRGB color surface and a linear packed map (red height, green roughness). Lossless WebP derivatives use the existing FFmpeg/libwebp installation. Source PNG derivatives remain separate from runtime files; [build-manifest.json](build-manifest.json) records every file/hash/dimension. World-space sampling places one repeat every two metres; no sign, label or collision information is baked into these surfaces.
